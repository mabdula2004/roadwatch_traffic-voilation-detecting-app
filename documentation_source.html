1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Modern metropolitan roads suffer from an alarming rate of traffic violations—including signal jumping, illegal wrong-way navigation, commercial lane blockage, and hazardous driving—that overwhelm municipal traffic police departments. Traditional fixed-camera CCTV infrastructures suffer from high capital expenditure, widespread blind spots, and vulnerability to occlusion.

RoadWatch solves this dilemma by turning verified citizens into active observers. Instead of relying on unreliable, unverified social media complaints or fallible autonomous AI fines, RoadWatch establishes a high-integrity, human-in-the-loop verification pipeline. Citizens safely capture 10-second violation clips; the client extracts multi-angle keyframes and stamps immutable GPS/orientation telemetry; Vision AI models assist with vehicle and license plate recognition; and certified Traffic Enforcement Officers review the evidence on a zero-latency web portal before any official legal citation is sanctioned.

Core Architectural Guarantee: Human-in-the-Loop Adjudication
In compliance with constitutional due process and evidence admissibility standards, no automated fine is ever generated solely by Artificial Intelligence. The Vision AI serves strictly as an assistive parser; every citation requires the explicit review and digital signature of an authorized Traffic Enforcement Officer.

👥 System Application User Roles

The RoadWatch software platform is architected around two primary application user roles and one autonomous backend engine. Offending drivers are external subjects of citations and do not hold software accounts.

📱 1. Reporter (Citizen Mobile App) Role: reporter

Registered citizen observer capturing and submitting verified road violations via the mobile app.

  • Registers with Phone OTP & 13-digit Pakistani CNIC (SHA-256 hashed).
  • Launches 10-second Camera HUD with live GPS and sensor stamping.
  • Reviews and corrects AI-extracted license plates and claims.
  • Monitors personal submissions in the sealed Evidence Vault.
  • Earns civic bounty payouts (PKR) into the digital reward wallet.
  • Posts PKR 100 refundable appeal bonds on contested rejections.
  • Triggers emergency SOS quick-dial (Rescue 1122 / Police 15).
👮 2. Traffic Police Officer (Command Dashboard) Role: officer

Certified municipal enforcement personnel operating the unified Command Audit Web Portal.

  • Receives real-time incoming violations via zero-latency WebSockets.
  • Streams in-browser MP4 videos directly from the secure Evidence Vault.
  • Examines 3 high-resolution extracted keyframes for plate legibility.
  • Cross-checks satellite GPS coordinates, timestamp, and reporter notes.
  • Executes 1-click Accept (issues fine, credits wallet, boosts score).
  • Executes 1-click Reject with reason tag (penalizes fake reports).
  • Adjudicates Citizen Dispute Appeals under the Disputed Cases room.
  • Audits closed citations and exports official CSV logs for courts.
  • Monitors Citizen Reporter Registry & trust reputation scores.
  • Utilizes rapid keyboard hotkeys (A, R, Space, J, K).
Note on Traffic Violators (Offenders):
The vehicle owner or driver who commits the violation is the external recipient of official traffic citations. Violators do not have application user accounts, screens, or login interfaces in the RoadWatch software; their citations are officially processed by the municipal traffic police department.

🔄 End-to-End Operational Workflow

The lifecycle of a traffic violation report traverses five distinct stages, transitioning seamlessly from native mobile hardware capture to cloud analysis, officer adjudication, and financial reconciliation.

1
Stage 1: Citizen Mobile Capture & Hardware Stamping
The citizen launches the native Camera HUD. The application enforces a strict 10-second recording limiter (with a 50MB ceiling) to preserve focused evidence. Concurrently, the mobile device samples high-precision GPS coordinates, capture timestamp, and gyro orientation angles. Immediately upon recording termination, the app extracts 3 multi-angle keyframes (frame_1.jpg, frame_2.jpg, frame_3.jpg) via video thumbnailing algorithms.
Hardware HUD GPS Stamping 3x Keyframes
2
Stage 2: Vision AI Inference & Pre-Screening
Extracted keyframes are processed through Vision AI inference models. The model identifies the vehicle category (Car, Motorcycle, Commercial Bus, Rickshaw, Heavy Truck), classifies violation categories (Red Light, Wrong-Way, Illegal Parking, Triple Riding, Helmet Violation), and performs Optical Character Recognition (OCR) on vehicle registration plates. The citizen reviews the AI prediction and attaches contextual notes.
Vehicle Classifier Plate OCR Confidence Score
3
Stage 3: Secure Cloud Ingestion & Evidence Vault Storage
A unique RFC4122 v4 UUID is generated as the client_capture_id. The MP4 video file and JPEG keyframes are transmitted over encrypted TLS to Supabase Storage's private evidence_vault bucket. An atomic transaction inserts metadata into public.reports with initial status pending_manual_review. Supabase's Realtime engine broadcasts the new insertion event across WebSocket channels.
UUID v4 evidence_vault (S3) WebSocket Broadcast
4
Stage 4: Officer Command Verification & Adjudication
The report instantly populates the Officer Command Dashboard's Live Pending Queue without page refresh. Cases are prioritized by severity (CRITICAL for Red Light/Wrong Way, HIGH for Reckless Driving). The officer opens the integrated Evidence Stage, plays the full MP4 video, toggles between high-res keyframes, and verifies the plate and GPS location. The officer adjudicates via hotkeys:
• Accept Citation (A): Marks report approved, schedules official e-challan, credits citizen bounty (PKR 250), increments citizen Trust Score (+2).
• Reject Citation (R): Dismisses report with reason tag. If deemed fraudulent, penalizes citizen Trust Score (-15).
Hotkeys: A / R In-Browser Player Trust Score Logic
5
Stage 5: Citizen Dispute Appeal & Re-Audit Protocol
If an honest citizen's submission is rejected, they are notified with the officer's reason. The citizen can initiate a formal Dispute Appeal by depositing a PKR 100 refundable bond and providing written contestation. The report transitions to pending_manual_review under the dashboard's "Disputed Cases" tab for officer re-audit. If the rejection is overturned, the PKR 100 deposit is refunded, the bounty is awarded, and the trust penalty is reversed.

🛡️ Privacy, Integrity & Anti-Fraud Engine

1. Privacy-Preserving Citizen Identity (NADRA CNIC Digest)

To safeguard citizen reporters against retaliation, RoadWatch enforces strict privacy standards. The mobile application never stores 13-digit National Identity Card (CNIC) numbers in plaintext on device storage or databases.

  • The raw CNIC is converted client-side into an HMAC-salted SHA-256 cryptographic digest before transmission.
  • Only the last four digits (e.g. •••••-•••••••-7412) are preserved for citizen account reference.
  • This ensures irreversible identity hashing, completely preventing identity theft or unauthorized police lookup of reporting citizens.

2. Dynamic Trust Score & Anti-Spam Game Theory

To counteract the classic vulnerability of crowd-sourced platforms—false reports, prank submissions, and grudge filings—RoadWatch deploys an automated Dynamic Trust Scoring Engine:

Event Condition Score Adjustment System Consequence
New Citizen Registration Baseline: 50 pts Standard review priority; standard submission limits.
Officer Accepts Valid Violation +2 pts Bounty credited (PKR 250); reputation elevated towards High-Trust tier.
Officer Rejects Inconclusive/Blurry 0 pts (Neutral) No penalty applied; educational feedback provided to reporter.
Officer Rejects Malicious / Fake Claim -15 pts Substantial penalty; account flagged on officer registry for suspension.
Trust Score Drops Below 20 pts AUTOMATIC SUSPENSION Submission privileges revoked; requires manual appeal to reinstate.
Trust Score Reaches 85+ pts FAST-TRACK TIER Expedited review queue placement; eligible for civic reward multipliers.

3. Hardware-Bound Evidence Integrity

  • UUID v4 Evidence Keys: Submissions are tagged with cryptographically generated RFC4122 v4 UUIDs, preventing collision or spoofing.
  • Location Fencing: GPS satellite coordinates and compass headings are sampled exclusively during the active 10-second capture window and permanently stamped to the evidence packet.
  • Immutable Storage Vault: Storage objects in evidence_vault are write-once and can only be deleted or altered by database administrator roles.
